Our History

Ceramica Cleopatra FCW was established to champion women's sports and provide a professional environment for female athletes in Egypt. Supported by the club's robust infrastructure, the team aims to become a dominant force in Egyptian women's football, competing fiercely at the national level.

🚀

Our Vision & Mission

Our mission is to empower young women through football, fostering talent, discipline, and teamwork. We envision a future where Egyptian women's football competes at the highest global standards, and Ceramica Cleopatra is at the forefront of that movement.
